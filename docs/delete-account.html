WatchVerse Account Deletion

You can delete your WatchVerse account directly in the app.

Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open WatchVerse.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm deletion.
  6. Reauthenticate if requested for security.

What Is Deleted

On Android, deleting your WatchVerse account removes your Firebase Authentication account and the associated WatchVerse account data stored in Cloud Firestore.

Associated WatchVerse data includes:

  • Watched and skipped progress
  • Badge progress and unlocks
  • App settings and preferences
  • WatchVerse profile and account data

Deleting your WatchVerse account does not delete your Google Account, Apple Account, or unrelated data held by Google or Apple.

Need Help?
