WatchVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Overview

WatchVerse respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the iOS and Android apps may collect, why it is collected, and how it is used to provide WatchVerse features.

Information We Collect

WatchVerse uses Sign in with Apple on iOS. On Android, you can use Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple. Depending on the provider and the information you choose to share, WatchVerse may receive account or profile information such as your name, email address, and a unique authentication identifier. WatchVerse does not receive your Apple or Google password.

WatchVerse stores information related to your use of the app, including:

  • Your account identifier and basic profile information
  • Your watched and skipped titles and journey progress
  • Your badge progress, unlocked badges, and displayed badge notifications
  • Your app settings, preferences, and selected filters

How Your Information Is Used

This information is used only to create and maintain your WatchVerse account, provide app features, restore your progress and settings, unlock badges, and synchronize your WatchVerse experience on supported devices and platforms.

iCloud and CloudKit

WatchVerse uses Apple's CloudKit service to store and synchronize account and progress information. CloudKit is operated by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy and security practices.

Firebase and Google Services on Android

On Android, WatchVerse uses Firebase Authentication to sign you in and identify your account. Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple are available authentication options. WatchVerse uses Cloud Firestore to store and synchronize your account profile, watched and skipped progress, badge information, and app settings. Firebase and Google services are operated by Google and are subject to Google's privacy and security practices.

Analytics and Advertising

WatchVerse does not use third-party advertising networks and does not sell personal data.

WatchVerse does not currently use third-party analytics services.

Data Sharing

WatchVerse does not sell or rent your personal data. Information is processed only as needed to provide WatchVerse functionality and synchronization through Apple CloudKit on iOS or Firebase and Google services on Android.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account and app information may remain stored while your WatchVerse account is active. iOS account data is stored through CloudKit. Android account data is stored through Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

You can delete your account from within the WatchVerse app. Deleting an account removes the associated WatchVerse account data according to the platform and backend implementation, including the applicable CloudKit data on iOS or Firebase Authentication account and Cloud Firestore data on Android.

Children's Privacy

WatchVerse is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as WatchVerse evolves. Changes will be reflected on this page.
