WatchVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Overview

WatchVerse respects your privacy. This policy explains what information WatchVerse collects, why it is collected, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

WatchVerse uses Sign in with Apple to create and identify your account. Depending on the information you choose to share through Sign in with Apple, WatchVerse may receive your name and a unique account identifier.

WatchVerse stores information related to your use of the app, including:

  • Your watched and skipped titles
  • Your journey progress
  • Your unlocked badges
  • Your app settings and selected filters

How Your Information Is Used

This information is used only to provide WatchVerse features, restore your progress, and synchronize your experience between supported Apple devices.

iCloud and CloudKit

WatchVerse uses Apple's CloudKit service to store and synchronize account and progress information. CloudKit is operated by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy and security practices.

Analytics and Advertising

WatchVerse does not use third-party advertising networks and does not sell personal information.

WatchVerse does not currently use third-party analytics services.

Data Sharing

WatchVerse does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Information is only processed as necessary to provide the application's features and Apple services used by the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account and progress information may remain stored in CloudKit while your WatchVerse account is active.

To request deletion of your WatchVerse account and associated data, contact:

maxp_dev@icloud.com

Children's Privacy

WatchVerse is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as WatchVerse evolves. Changes will be reflected on this page.
